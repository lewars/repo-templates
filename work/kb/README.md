# Knowledge Base (kb)

A personal work repository for capturing technical insights, operational procedures, and architectural brainstorming.

## 📂 Structure

- **architecture/**: Design documents and future-state solutions.
- **how-to/**: Standard operating procedures and "cheatsheets."
- **troubleshooting/**: Post-mortems and quick fixes for known issues.
- **library/**: General research and notes on specific tooling (Linux, K8s, Python).
- **journal/**: Scratchpad for daily observations.

## 🛠 Usage

- **Editor**: Emacs (optimized for Org-mode and Markdown).
- **Workflow**: 
  1. Capture thoughts in `journal/`.
  2. Refactor useful patterns into `how-to/` or `library/`.
  3. Commit and push frequently to maintain a versioned history of learning.
